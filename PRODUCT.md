# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Signal serves founders, marketing leads, and small teams at startups and small
companies that have no dedicated SEO hire. They own one or more verified websites,
usually a marketing site or blog in a GitHub repository. They check in briefly:
reviewing what Signal shipped and measured, deciding the items Signal escalated (in
the dashboard, Slack, or Telegram), adjusting how much Signal may do on its own, and
reading the weekly report. They should never need to read infrastructure logs.

## Product Purpose

Signal is an autonomous, evidence-backed SEO and AI-search employee. It learns the
business, researches, plans, writes articles, fixes technical SEO, ships changes
through pull requests, verifies what went live, measures the effect, and reports
every week under a human-granted standing authorization. Success means organic
traffic and AI-assistant citations grow while authority, confidence, uncertainty,
missing data, and external outcomes remain explicit.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, and candid. Signal should feel attentive and capable without
performing certainty, urgency, or artificial enthusiasm.

## Anti-references

- Marketing-style SaaS dashboards with oversized hero copy and ornamental metrics.
- Black-box AI interfaces that hide evidence, decisions, costs, or approval scope.
- Dense infrastructure consoles that require operators to translate system jargon.
- Decorative purple gradients, glass panels, excessive cards, and novelty controls.
- Green-success interfaces that use color alone or imply completion before proof.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with current truth: state, freshness, blockers, and uncertainty come before
   decoration or recommendations.
2. Keep authority visible: users can always distinguish observation, proposal,
   approval, execution, verification, and recovery.
3. Make evidence inspectable: important claims connect to their source and time.
4. Prefer operational calm: repeated workflows should be compact, predictable, and
   easy to scan.
5. Show autonomy honestly: users can always see what Signal did on its own, which
   authorization allowed it, how confident it was, and what is waiting for them.
6. Never simulate readiness: unavailable connectors or workflows remain visibly
   unavailable until their real contracts are implemented and qualified.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must support keyboard operation, visible focus,
screen-reader labels, non-color status cues, sufficient contrast, reduced motion,
readable errors, and accessible tables, diffs, dialogs, and charts. Missing data
and disabled authority must be understandable without relying on visual styling.
